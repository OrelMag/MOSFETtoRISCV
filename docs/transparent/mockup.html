Transparent boxes: zooming reveals what is inside

Reading of the request. The author wrote "transport boxes"; this mockup reads it as transparent boxes (CLAUDE.md: "every component on screen is a transparent box"). Correct me if "transport" meant something else.

Mockup only: nothing here is in the product. Every circuit picture is the site's own renderer (SchematicView + svgImage, built from docs/transparent/proto) of the single-cycle RISC-V CPU after six clock cycles of fib, and of its alu (alu32) with the same live values. The lens frame, the connectors, the shading and the phone chrome are drawn over them by hand. Decisions are in DESIGN.md.

1. The recommended behaviour: a lens grows out of the box

Zoom toward a block. Once it is big enough on screen (96 px tall), a lens opens over it: the block's own drawing with live values, at a scale where its block names read. The rest of the circuit stays, dimmed. Zoom further and the lens grows with the zoom. When it would fill the screen, the view becomes that level (a drill), from the same picture. Zoom out and it closes.

2. The shape problem, and three answers side by side

The ALU's symbol is 8 × 12 grid units; its inside is 78 × 146, so 12 times larger. Over the 66 boxes on the three CPU tops, an inside is a median 7.5× its symbol (p90 54×, a 187-bit register 250×), and the shapes differ by a median factor of 3 in aspect (p90 28). A 32-bit register is a tall column of 32 bits behind a small square.

Budget: the route cache ships first (phase 0), for drill as much as for the lens. While a zoom gesture runs, nothing is built: the lens only moves and fades (≤ 2 ms a frame). When the gesture settles, the level's wires come from a route cache made at build time (like hwstats.json); a level not in it is routed then. Its DOM is built off screen in its own SchematicView, as image.ts's exportInside already draws a part's inside, in one task under 50 ms, and swapped in. No worker: routeNetlist takes ComponentDefs, which carry functions. Measured on a desktop shared with other sessions' builds; headless Chrome with --disable-gpu; Node v22.13.1.
